# Portrait Photography

## Overview
Individual, couple, family, and professional headshot portrait sessions, shot either
in-studio or on-location (park, downtown, client's home/office).

## Packages
- Individual/Headshot Session (30 min, in-studio or on-location): $150 - $250,
  10-15 edited digital photos.
- Couple/Maternity/Family Session (45-60 min): $250 - $400, 20-30 edited digital
  photos.
- Extended Family / Group Session (60-90 min, up to 10 people): $400 - $600,
  30-40 edited digital photos.
- Corporate headshot packages (per person, on-site for teams of 5+): $75 - $120/person,
  minimum group booking required.
Pricing varies with location, group size, and number of outfit/backdrop changes.

## Typical Process
1. Booking request through the website or this chat assistant (date, session type,
   location preference).
2. Confirmation with a 20% deposit to hold the time slot.
3. Session takes place at the studio or agreed outdoor/on-location spot.
4. Online proofing gallery delivered within 3-5 business days for client to select
   favorites (if package includes selection-based editing).
5. Final edited gallery delivered within 1-2 weeks.

## What to Expect / Prep Tips
- Studio sessions include access to basic backdrops (white, gray, black) and
  professional lighting.
- Outdoor sessions are weather-dependent; free rescheduling for rain/unsafe conditions.
- Clients may bring up to 2 outfit changes for standard sessions at no extra cost.

## Booking
This chat assistant can collect session type, preferred date/time, location
preference, and contact info to submit a booking request.
